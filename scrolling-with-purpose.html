Scrolling With Purpose: Using TikTok to Learn about AI in the Elementary Classroom

🌻
Lindsey S.
4th Grade Teacher · Ed.D. Candidate, Instructional Technology
May 2026 · 4 min read

Social media sourcing is the intentional practice of searching and curating content from social media platforms for professional growth. For educators, this means going beyond passive scrolling to deliberately discover tools, strategies and ideas that transfer directly to classroom instruction. TikTok, one of the fastest-growing social media platforms in the world, has emerged as an unexpected but powerful space where educators are increasingly sharing and discovering classroom-ready ideas.

(Generated with user input in Anthropic, 2026)

Social media platforms cultivate collaboration, communication and active participation in learning. This informal network leads to increased engagement and learning opportunities outside of professional channels (Alshalawi, 2022; García-Río et al., 2022).

The infographic above highlights a focused TikTok search on a specific topic: how elementary teachers are introducing artificial intelligence (AI) tools to students. Using hashtags like #AIinEducation, #AIinElementary and #AIschools, I identified three accounts that are adding to the conversation around AI in schools. @edutechwizard (Andrew Davies) shares student facing AI tools and practical ed tech strategies for grades K-12. @MagicSchool shows AI tools built specifically for K-12 teachers, and how to build skills on the student-facing platform. @briskteaching focuses on AI writing feedback, a real issue for educators trying to give meaningful and timely feedback to student writing.

In a recent survey of 101 K-12 teachers using TikTok as a professional learning tool, three out of four educators implemented TikTok ideas directly into the classroom (Nochumson, 2024). Here are a few ways to get the most out of TikTok as a professional learning tool: search a specific topic rather than browsing your for you page. Save and categorize your content intentionally so it can be used like a resource library. Also, use TikTok to inspire you and take action. Make a plan for how to take ideas saved in a video into your classroom.

"Teachers are learning from each other on TikTok every day. The key is knowing how to find that conversation intentionally."
